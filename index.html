Every ant only senses what is directly around it — no ant knows the way. Keys: Space run · → step · R reset · 1–5 editor tools.

How it works?

  1. 1

    Ants explore. Each ant wanders using only local senses: a few samples of pheromone around its head.

  2. 2

    Some find food. By pure chance, one ant reaches a food source and picks up a piece.

  3. 3

    They mark the way back. Carrying food, the ant walks home and deposits a food pheromone trail behind it.

  4. 4

    Others follow the strongest signal. Searching ants sample left / centre / right and turn toward the stronger trail.

  5. 5

    Pheromones evaporate. Every step the field fades, so old and weak paths lose influence.

  6. 6

    Weak routes disappear. Long, unlucky routes get less traffic, fade faster and are abandoned.

  7. 7

    Stable highways emerge. Short routes collect more ants, more pheromone and stay strong. No one planned it — it self-organizes.
